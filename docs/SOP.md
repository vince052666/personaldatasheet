# Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

## PDS Management System - Philippine Government Agencies

### Encoding Procedures
Ensure accurate and complete encoding of Personal Data Sheets (PDS) into the system.

**Quality Standards:**
- Accuracy: 99.5% - Maximum of 1 error per 200 data points
- Completeness: 100% - All required fields must be filled
- Timeliness: Encode within 2 working days of receipt

### Review Procedures
Validate accuracy and completeness of encoded PDS records.

**SLA:**
- Review Time: Within 1 working day of submission
- Feedback Quality: Clear, specific, actionable

### Approval Procedures
Final authorization of PDS records for official use by Agency Administrator/HR Chief.

### Corrections Procedures
Handle corrections to approved PDS records with proper audit trail.

### Exception Handling
Common exceptions: Missing Documents, Conflicting Information, System Issues, Import Errors, Legal Hold, DPA Requests

**Escalation Path:**
1. HR Encoder/Reviewer
2. HR Chief
3. Agency Administrator
4. IT Support/Legal Officer
5. CSC/DPA/COA (external)
